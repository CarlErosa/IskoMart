# IskoMart

A marketplace for PUP students. Buy, sell, and ask for what you need inside the campus
community — with a real name and a school email behind every account.

## What you can do

- List an item with photos, price, category, condition, and a meetup spot
- Post a "looking for" request when you need something
- Browse and search the feed, filter by category, like, repost, and comment
- Message a buyer or seller directly in the app
- Save your favourite finds to your Likes
- Report a listing or block an account you don't want to see
- Reset your password and keep your profile up to date

## How it works

1. Sign up with your personal Gmail address and verify it.
2. Post your listing or request. Photos, price, and meetup details go in one place.
3. Chat in the app to agree on the details.
4. Meet on campus and pay in person.

Every transaction happens face to face. IskoMart does not handle payments, shipping, or
delivery.

## Ground rules

- One account per student, verified through Gmail.
- Meet in public, well-lit places on campus.
- List only what you actually own and can hand over.
- Keep chat about the deal; report anything that feels off.
- The full rules and privacy notice are linked in the site footer.

## Built with

Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase, deployed on Vercel.
