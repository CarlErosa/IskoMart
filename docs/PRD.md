# IskoMart Product Requirements

## Assumptions

- PUP students verify accounts with an approved `@pup.edu.ph` email address.
- Users can be buyers, sellers, or both through one account.
- Seller contact is in-app chat.
- Buyers meet sellers and pay in person; online payments and delivery are not part of MVP.

## Problem

PUP students currently discover and sell goods through informal lagoon stalls and scattered
Freedom Wall posts. These channels make listings difficult to search, seller identity and
reputation difficult to assess, and conversations easy to lose.

## Target Users

- PUP students looking for affordable products from other students.
- PUP students who want a trusted storefront for products they sell on campus.

## MVP Scope

- PUP student account registration and verification.
- A seller storefront with profile information and listings.
- Product listings with photos, title, description, price, category, and availability.
- Browse, search, and category filtering.
- In-app messaging between buyers and sellers.
- Seller ratings and reviews after a transaction.
- Meetup coordination and payment in person.

## Non-goals

- Delivery, shipping, or courier tracking.
- Online payments, escrow, refunds, or order checkout.
- Admin dashboard or full moderation tooling.
- Non-PUP account access.

## User Stories

- As a PUP student, I can register with my PUP email so the marketplace remains student-only.
- As a buyer, I can browse and search listings by category to find relevant products.
- As a buyer, I can view a seller storefront, listings, and ratings before contacting them.
- As a buyer, I can message a seller in-app to arrange a meetup and ask questions.
- As a seller, I can create and manage a storefront and product listings with photos.
- As a seller, I can mark a listing unavailable when it is no longer for sale.
- As a buyer, I can leave a rating and review for a seller after a completed meetup.
- As a marketplace participant, I can transact in person without entering payment details online.
